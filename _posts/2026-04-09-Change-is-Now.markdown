(Life until today)

The past week felt like a reflection of my whole year. I’ve been lazy for a long time, and it’s starting to show in my work. But this last week hit differently—I’ve lost confidence. I feel demotivated. Like a lazy bastard who’s just drifting. Spent hours watching reels, trying to feel motivated. Came across Richard Feynman — and something stuck with me. He said anyone can think the way he thinks, if they’re willing to put in the effort, devote time, and truly work hard. There are no “magic people.”

Maybe it’s finally time for me to change.

I don’t want to be a loser anymore. No more laziness. Time to grind. It’s going to be hard, I know that. But I’m ready to put in the effort. God, nature, and all the great minds I look up to—watch over me. I’m willing to work.
 
From tomorrow, I want to become a new person.

Like Hermann Hesse wrote in Siddhartha: to think, to fast, and to wait.

This will be my motto.

“I can think. I can wait. I can fast.”

This time, I don’t just want to say it—I want to live it.
